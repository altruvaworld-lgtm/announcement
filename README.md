OFFICIAL CREATOR STATEMENT

ALTRUVA – SYSTEM CLARIFICATION, TRANSPARENCY & FUTURE DIRECTION

A Message from the Creator of Altruva

Dear Altruva Global Community,

First and foremost, I would like to express my sincere appreciation to every member, supporter, leader, and contributor who has been part of the Altruva journey.

I want to address some important decisions regarding the system structure, recent changes, and the future direction of Altruva.

1. The Original System Was Built with Research and Planning

When Altruva was initially developed, its system was designed through extensive planning, research, development (R&D), calculations, and long-term projections.

Every component of the original system had a purpose. It was not designed randomly or without consideration. It was developed with a clear vision of sustainability, transparency, decentralization, and long-term community benefits.

Unfortunately, within a relatively short period, certain members of the core team and top leadership strongly pushed for changes to the original system.

As the Creator, I eventually had to make changes under considerable pressure, despite having a different long-term vision.

I believe that forcing significant structural changes to a carefully researched system without sufficient time, evaluation, and collective understanding was not the right approach.

The consequences were also reflected in business performance and community confidence.

2. The Creator's Position Going Forward

I want to make one thing absolutely clear:

The future direction of Altruva will be guided by the original vision, research, planning, and long-term strategy of its Creator.

No individual leader or core team member will have the authority to repeatedly influence or force changes to the fundamental system structure.

Suggestions and constructive feedback will always be respected. However, system-level decisions must be based on proper evaluation, sustainability, and the overall interests of the community.

Community support is important to the Creator, but decisions must never be driven by pressure or individual interests.

3. Returning to the Original System Structure

After reviewing the situation, the decision is to return to the original system structure, with only the necessary adjustments outlined below.

The original vision and fundamental distribution philosophy will remain intact.

The following updates will apply:

- The existing Jackpot concept will continue, with its presentation/name updated to Mystery Pool.
- The Nexus structure will remain in place.
- No additional rank structures will be introduced.
- The previous overall system philosophy and planned structure will be maintained.

4. Jackpot / Mystery Pool Distribution Update

The distribution structure will be adjusted as follows:

Previous Structure| Updated Structure
$4,000 Distribution| $2,000 Distribution
250 Winners| 125 Winners
5% Allocation| 4% Allocation

The distribution will now be made among 125 winners instead of 250 winners, with a $2,000 distribution amount for this allocation.

The remaining 1% from the previous 5% allocation will be redirected to the Nexus Pool.

Therefore, the revised allocation will be:

- 4% – Mystery Pool Distribution
- 1% – Nexus Pool

This is the specific adjustment to the allocation structure. The original overall vision and distribution philosophy will otherwise remain unchanged.

5. Nexus Eligibility – A Fair Opportunity for $25 Members

Another important decision concerns the $25 package.

I believe that every genuine community participant deserves a fair opportunity to progress.

A $25 participant should not automatically be excluded from Nexus eligibility simply because they selected a smaller package.

Therefore, an additional eligibility option will be available:

$25 Package + 5 Direct Referrals

This option will allow eligible $25 participants to qualify for the applicable Nexus structure, subject to the system's defined conditions.

We want to create opportunities based on participation and contribution rather than unnecessarily limiting smaller-package members.

6. The Community Is More Important Than Individual Positions

I want everyone to understand something important.

Altruva was never envisioned as a platform designed only for individual leaders or a particular group of people.

The community is the foundation of this entire vision.

The Creator has always believed in building a fully decentralized ecosystem with transparency and a distribution-oriented approach.

The original vision also included additional long-term plans through which the community would have opportunities to earn, while the Creator would have a sustainable business model through separately planned initiatives.

The Creator did not reserve a personal income allocation within the original distribution structure. The intention was to build the system around community participation, transparency, and decentralization.

There are still important plans and opportunities that have not yet been introduced.

7. Our Commitment Going Forward

From this point forward:

- The original system vision will remain the foundation.
- No repeated structural changes will be made under individual or leadership pressure.
- The Nexus structure will continue.
- The Mystery Pool distribution will follow the revised allocation.
- The $25 package will have an additional Nexus eligibility option.
- Transparency, consistency, and community interests will remain central to future decisions.

A system built through research should not be repeatedly redesigned without proper evaluation.

A strong ecosystem requires consistency, trust, and a clear vision.

FINAL MESSAGE FROM THE CREATOR

I respect every member of this community, every genuine leader, and everyone who has contributed to Altruva.

However, respect for the community must also mean protecting the original vision and the long-term sustainability of the system.

Leadership is important, suggestions are valuable, and community feedback matters. But the fundamental direction of a project cannot continuously change according to the demands of a few individuals.

My responsibility as the Creator is to protect the vision, maintain the system's integrity, and work towards a sustainable future for the entire community.

ONE VISION. ONE SYSTEM. ONE COMMUNITY.

Let's move forward with clarity, transparency, consistency, and confidence.

Regards,

The Creator
